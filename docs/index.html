🧪 Build your buyer panel

Pick your markets, how many people from each, their genders, ages, incomes, habits and biases. Everything is custom. Then let the Founder skill in Claude show your idea to every one of them.

1. 💡 Your business

What you sell, to whom, and the exact pitch every buyer sees.

2. 🌍 Markets and headcount

Add each market (country or region) and how many buyers to take from it. Each market gets its own currency, local names, and official facts about how people there pay and save.

3. 👫 Gender

Percentages across all buyers. Leave empty to not set gender.

4. 🎂 Age groups

Cover every age that really buys.

5. 🛒 How they buy

Buying habits, with the share of buyers who shop like that.

6. 🤔 Doubts and daily life

7. 🧩 Your own groups

Any other split you have real numbers for: city tier, diet, ethnicity, religion, home type, car owned... Buyers are always told to act as one person, never a stereotype.

8. 🧠 Biases

Real, well-studied ways people decide. Each buyer gets one, spread evenly.

9. 📰 Real facts and reviews (recommended)

Facts about how people in your market buy, and real reviews of competitors. Every one needs a source link: buyers are told these are real. Never invent one.

⬇️ Get your files

  1. Install the skill: paste github.com/Siddharthmehdiratta/founder-skill-plus into Claude and say "install skill".
  2. Put both files in a founder folder in your project.
  3. In Claude, type /founder-consumer and say "use my founder/customer.json and founder/pitch.md". Start with 20 buyers for a cheap first look.
  4. Open results.html for the full interactive report.
