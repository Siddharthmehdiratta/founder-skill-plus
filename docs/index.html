What AI customers can, and can't, tell you.

Good for

Finding objections, questions and price ranges early, and picking between two pitches before you spend.

Read as a ceiling

AI customers lean agreeable, so treat the buy rate as the best case. The reasons and doubts matter more than the number.

Not a replacement

Confirm with real people next: a waitlist, a pre-sale, or a price on a page. The report tells you what to test.
