Customer research in an afternoon, not a month.

Three steps. About ten minutes.

  1. 1

    Pick your customers

    Start from your industry, or build your panel from scratch. Every choice is a dropdown.

  2. 2

    Copy one message

    Press Copy the message for Claude, paste it into the Claude app or claude.ai, and send it.

  3. 3

    Read your score

    Claude plays every customer, then writes your score, the reasons, what to fix and your sales replies.

What AI customers can, and can't, tell you.

Good for

Finding objections, questions and price ranges early, and picking between two pitches before you spend.

Read as a ceiling

AI customers lean agreeable, so treat the buy rate as the best case. The reasons and doubts matter more than the number.

Not a replacement

Confirm with real people next: a waitlist, a pre-sale, or a price on a page. The report tells you what to test.
