What you get

Customer research in an afternoon, not a month

Use it to find objections and pick between pitches before you spend on ads, stock or a shop. Then confirm with real people.

Customers you choose

Markets, cities, incomes, ages, genders, buying habits and well-studied biases, with real facts about how each market pays and saves.

Reactions in their own words

Each buyer says yes or no, why, what would change their mind, and the questions they'd ask, in English, Hinglish, Hindi and more.

A score and what to fix

A 0 to 100 score from demand, price fit and trust, the groups that respond best, and the fixes ranked by how many buyers they'd win.

A sales kit you can use

Answers to the questions buyers ask most, price ranges they'd accept, and replies to their doubts, ready for your page or pitch.

How it works

Three steps, about ten minutes

  1. 1

    Pick your customers

    Start from your industry below, or build your panel from scratch. Every choice is a dropdown.

  2. 2

    Copy one message

    Press Run it in Claude, then paste the message into the Claude app or claude.ai and send it.

  3. 3

    Read your score

    Claude plays every customer and writes your score, the reasons, what to fix and your sales replies.
