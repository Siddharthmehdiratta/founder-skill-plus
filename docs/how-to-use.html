How to use Founder Plus

A beginner's guide. No coding needed. About 20 minutes the first time, 5 minutes after that.

What it does, in one line

You describe your business idea and pick the customers you want to sell to. AI customers react to it like real people would, and you get a report on who would buy, what holds people back, the questions they'd ask, and the price that feels right.

The quick way: no installs (5 minutes)

1

Pick your customers

Open the customer builder. Choose your industry from the menu at the top, then change the pitch and customers to match yours.

2

Copy the message

In the Run it in Claude box, choose how many customers (20 is best) and press Copy the message for Claude.

3

Paste it into Claude

Open the Claude app or claude.ai, paste, and send. In a few minutes you get every customer's answer, a score out of 100, a plain summary, a detailed summary and a sales answer kit (WhatsApp replies, FAQ and a sales call script).

The quick way works on Claude's free plan too, though long answers may hit the free limits: 10 customers is safer there. For bigger panels, separate AI customers and the full interactive report, use the full way below.

The full way: step by step

1
3 minutes

Pick your customers

Open the customer builder. Tip: choose your industry from the Start from your industry menu at the top to fill everything in for you. Then type your idea and price, then choose who your customers are: where they live, age, gender, income and how they shop. Add as many groups as you like. Selling across India or the UK? One tap adds city tiers (Tier 1 metros to Tier 4 villages), household income tiers (Tier 1 to Tier 4) or London vs the rest of the UK, with published, linked facts on how each group spends.

Press Download customer.json and Download pitch.md. You now have 2 files.

2
1 minute

Make a folder for your idea

On your computer, make a new folder, for example my-idea. Inside it, make another folder called founder. Move your 2 downloaded files into the founder folder.

my-idea └─ founder ├─ customer.json └─ pitch.md
3
5 minutes

Open Claude Desktop in "Code"

Download and install the Claude Desktop app from claude.ai/download. Sign in with your Pro (or higher) plan.

Open the Code tab and choose your my-idea folder.

4
2 minutes, only the first time

Install Founder Plus

Copy this message, paste it into Claude and press Enter:

Install this skill pack for me: https://github.com/Siddharthmehdiratta/founder-skill-plus

Say yes when Claude asks for permission. When it says it is done, close and reopen the Code tab.

5
5 to 10 minutes

Run your customer test

Copy this message, paste it into Claude and press Enter:

/founder-consumer --size small Use founder/customer.json and founder/pitch.md.

Claude will show your idea to 20 AI customers, a few at a time. Approve when it asks to save their answers.

Tip: each AI customer uses some of your Claude plan's usage. Start with 20. Go bigger (--size medium = 100) once you like the setup.
6
As long as you like

Open your report

Open your my-idea folder, then founder → panel, and double-click results.html. It opens in your browser.

Start with the Score & summary tab, then What to do next. Your score, plain summary and detailed summary are also saved as audit.md; ask Claude to "write the sales kit" for WhatsApp replies, an FAQ and a call script. The Reviews they'd leave tab shows the stars, reviews and recommendation score (NPS) buyers expect to give. Then check Questions to answer: those are the questions real buyers will ask you.

If something goes wrong

Claude says "/founder-consumer" is not a command

The install did not finish, or the Code tab needs a restart. Repeat step 4, then close and reopen the Code tab. If you installed it as a plugin, the command is /founder-skill:founder-consumer.

Claude says Python is missing

Install Python 3 from python.org. On Windows, tick "Add python.exe to PATH" during install. Then try again. If Claude says python3 is not found, tell it: "use python instead of python3".

It keeps asking me to approve every file

That is normal: each AI customer saves one answer file. You can switch Claude to accept edits automatically for this folder while the test runs.

I hit my usage limit

Wait for the limit to reset, then type the same command again. It only runs the customers who have not answered yet.

Is this a real forecast of sales?

No. These are AI customers, not real ones. They are very good at finding objections, missing information and the questions buyers will ask. Before you spend real money, test the improved offer with 10 real people.
