How it works

How your AI customer panel works

What happens between pressing Build my panel and reading your score, and where the tool's limits are.

  1. 1

    You describe the offer

    Your business, the price, and the pitch exactly as a buyer would see it. Start from a ready setup for your industry.

  2. 2

    You choose the customers

    Markets, headcount, genders, ages, incomes, buying habits and biases. Every choice is a dropdown or a number.

  3. 3

    Claude plays every customer

    Each AI customer reads your pitch and answers as one person: yes or no, why, their doubts, and what would change their mind.

Real facts, not guesses

Each customer is built from published data

Markets come with official facts about how people there pay and save: for example household spending from India's MoSPI survey, income tiers from the ICE 360 survey, or ONS figures for the UK. Facts carry a source link, and customers are told which facts are real.

  • Incomes, ages and genders spread across the panel in the shares you set
  • Buying habits like "reads every review" or "free-tier forever"
  • One well-studied bias per customer, such as loss aversion or social proof
  • Answers in English, Hinglish, Hindi, German, Mandarin and more
The score

How the 0 to 100 score is made

The score compares versions of your idea on the same panel. It is not a sales forecast.

PartPointsWhat it measures
Demand60How keen buyers are (1 to 5) and how many said yes
Price fit20How many find your price below their "too expensive" point
Trust20How few hold back because they don't trust it yet
Honest limits

What AI customers can and can't tell you

Good for

Finding objections, questions and price ranges early, and picking between two pitches before you spend.

Read as a ceiling

AI customers lean agreeable, so treat the buy rate as the best case. The reasons and doubts matter more than the number.

Not a replacement

Confirm with real people next: a waitlist, a pre-sale, or a price on a page. The report tells you what to test.
