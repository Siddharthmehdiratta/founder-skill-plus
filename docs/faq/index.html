Getting started

Is it really free?+

Yes. Founder Plus is open source under the MIT licence. The quick test runs in the Claude app you already use, including the free plan.

Do I need to install anything?+

No. Build your panel on the website, press Copy the message for Claude, and paste it into the Claude app or claude.ai. Claude Code is only needed for bigger panels and the full interactive report.

How many AI customers should I use?+

20 is a good first look. 10 is faster and safer on Claude's free plan. Use 30 or more with Claude Code when you want steadier numbers.

Can I start from an example?+

Yes. Pick your industry at the top of the builder. It fills every field with a realistic setup that you can change.

The results

How accurate is it?+

It's research, not a forecast. AI customers lean agreeable, so read the buy rate as a ceiling. The value is in the reasons, doubts and questions, which you can then check with real people.

What does the score mean?+

0 to 100, made of Demand (60 points), Price fit (20) and Trust (20). Use it to compare two versions of your idea on the same panel.

Why do some customers answer in Hinglish or other languages?+

You choose the languages per market. Real customers in India often think and write in Hinglish, so their answers come back that way, with an English translation.

Customers and markets

Which markets have real facts built in?+

India, the United States, the United Kingdom, Europe, China and Japan come with published facts about income and how people pay. You can add your own facts with source links.

Can I test a business that sells to other businesses?+

Yes. Choose Businesses (B2B) and set the roles that decide, like owner, manager and finance, with what each cares about.

Why are gender and age dropdowns?+

So every panel adds up to 100% and the setup file stays valid. Leave gender empty if it doesn't matter for your product.

Privacy

Where does my business idea go?+

The builder runs in your browser and stores nothing. The message goes only where you paste it: your own Claude chat.

Can I save my setup?+

Yes. Download customer.json and open it again later with Open a saved setup.
